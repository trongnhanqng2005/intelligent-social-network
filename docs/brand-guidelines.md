# Weylume Brand Guidelines

## Identity

**Name:** Weylume
**Purpose:** a welcoming community identity centered on human connection,
shared interests and belonging.
**Technical project identifier:** `intelligent-social-network`.

The brand name was selected by the Project Owner. Trademark and domain
availability have not been verified.

## Mark and wordmark

The original Weylume symbol is an abstract W formed by two intersecting ribbon
strokes. Use the supplied vector mark and visible `Weylume` wordmark together in
the primary lockup. The compact symbol is intended for small navigation marks,
favicon and touch icon.

- Keep clear space around the symbol equal to at least half its rendered width.
- Keep the mark upright and at its supplied proportions.
- Prefer the teal mark on warm-white or white; use the reversed supplied SVG on
  dark teal when required.
- Do not recreate the mark from text, add effects, or substitute the retired
  Connected Nodes illustration.
- Check legibility at 16px, 24px and 40px before using the compact form.

## Visual language

- Warm editorial composition, calm negative space and human-scale typography.
- Warm-white canvas, neutral surfaces and deep teal action color.
- Be Vietnam Pro with system sans fallbacks; keep Vietnamese diacritics readable.
- Use original editorial scenes to express human connection: shared spaces,
  conversation and welcoming new beginnings. The illustrations may use restrained
  warm clay and sand tones as support for the established teal/neutral palette.
  Keep the Weylume W mark in the brand lockup; do not reuse an enlarged W, graph,
  pulse line or abstract crossing strokes as the main page illustration.
- Keep the homepage, returning-user and registration scenes related in palette
  and shape language but distinct in story. Human scenes are illustrative, not
  depictions of real users or evidence of current product activity. Do not add
  fake profile cards, feeds, metrics or testimonial photography.
- Use Lucide outline icons consistently for interface controls; the logo is not
  an interface icon set.

## Voice

Use natural Vietnamese copy that is confident, welcoming and specific about the
product's purpose. The brand may speak about connection and belonging without
claiming unimplemented social, AI or safety functionality. Do not use developer
progress notes or health status as public product messaging.

## Assets and licensing

- Source assets: `apps/web/public/brand/`.
- The Safari pinned-tab icon uses `weylume-mask.svg`, a monochrome mark with a
  transparent background; do not point `mask-icon` at the square-background
  browser favicon.
- Font files and SIL Open Font License: `apps/web/public/fonts/`.
- The current homepage and authentication scenes are original inline SVG in the
  React page/shell components; they use no external imagery or third-party asset
  license. Document source and attribution before adding any future photography
  or externally sourced illustration.
- Do not claim trademark/domain clearance. Review future third-party or
  generated assets for licensing and resemblance before adoption.
